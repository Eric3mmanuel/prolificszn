About

PROLIFIC szn

More ideas. More movement. More risk. More life. We are a Nairobi-born streetwear label built around expression. Our pieces are not designed to make you fit in. They're designed to become part of the story you're already writing. Every drop is a snapshot of a moment — the city, the pressure, the hype, the nights, the dreams.
